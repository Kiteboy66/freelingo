# Native isiXhosa lesson audio

The `unjani-speaker-1.mp3` and `unjani-speaker-2.mp3` files are format-converted
copies of the following utterances from the African Speech Technology isiXhosa
corpus distributed by OpenSLR as SLR32:

- `xho_4280_7361414109.wav` — “Unjani?”
- `xho_5378_5244558219.wav` — “Unjani?”

Source: <https://openslr.org/32/>

The source corpus was collected by the North-West University and Google teams
and is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. These derived
audio files are distributed under the same license:
